Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do pipe cleaner chenille bouquets and ruffled lilies last? +

Forever! Unlike real flowers that wither within days, our high-density chenille pipe cleaner flowers do not fade, wilt, or crumble. Simply keep them in a dry spot out of direct heavy moisture, and they will stay fluffy and vibrant for years as a treasured keepsake.

How do I place an order? +

You can browse our collections, build your custom arrangement with our on-page Bouquet Builder, and click "Order via WhatsApp" or "DM on Instagram". We will immediately confirm your flower preferences, delivery address, and payment details via UPI / Bank Transfer / Card.

Do you offer custom colors and personalized message cards? +

Yes, 100%! Customization is our specialty. You can choose custom petal colors, wrapper paper, ribbon textures, and provide a personal love note which we will handwrite on an aesthetic card for free.

Are fairy lights included with the bouquets? +

Yes, warm micro LED fairy lights are complimentary with all signature bouquets! They come with an easy-to-use mini battery switch tucked discretely beneath the satin ribbon.

How are the bouquets packaged for shipping so they don't get crushed? +

We pack every order inside rigid corrugated gift boxes with protective cushioning and bubble wrap. Even if slightly compressed during transit, the flexible inner wire allows you to gently reshape any petal to perfection in seconds!

What is the delivery timeline? +

Ready-to-ship designs take 2–3 business days. Bespoke custom arrangements take 3–5 business days to craft with love before dispatch. Express shipping options are available upon request.
